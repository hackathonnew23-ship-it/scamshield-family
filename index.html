            Digital arrest

            A caller says they are police or CBI and that you are under arrest on a video call. Red flag: they tell you to keep it secret and pay to clear your name. No agency does this.

            KYC and OTP

            A message says your account will be blocked unless you update KYC through a link. Red flag: any request for an OTP, PIN or CVV. Banks never ask for these.

            UPI collect request

            Someone says they sent money by mistake and asks you to approve a request to return it. Approving takes money out of your account. You never need a PIN to receive money.

            Fake job or investment

            Easy daily income or guaranteed returns for simple tasks. Red flag: you pay a small amount first, then more. Real jobs do not charge you to start.
